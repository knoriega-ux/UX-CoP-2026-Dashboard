UX CoP 2026 – Events & Mentoring

UX CoP 2026 events and mentoring metrics, designed to help UX professionals see how our community is growing and where we’re investing our time.

About this dashboard

This site summarizes 2026 UX Community of Practice events and 2024–2026 mentoring cohorts. The underlying data is available in uxcop_2024_2026_events_and_mentoring.csv in this repository.

Data overview

Data set Description File
Events & Mentoring (2024–2026) Includes UX CoP 2026 events (meetings, Creative Sips, networking) and mentoring cohorts I–III. uxcop_2024_2026_events_and_mentoring.csv

You can download the CSV and open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or Tableau to explore attendance, topics, and mentoring participation.
