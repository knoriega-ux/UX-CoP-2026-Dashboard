UX CoP 2026 – Events & Mentoring

UX CoP 2026 events and mentoring metrics, designed to help UX professionals see how our community is growing and where we’re investing our time.

2026 highlights (so far)

  • 11 UX CoP sessions with recorded attendance between January and August 2026.
  • 366 total attendees, averaging roughly 33 participants per session.
  • Mix of CoP meetings, Creative Sip sessions, and networking/mentoring events.

Mentoring program highlights (2024–2026)

  • 100 total participants across three cohorts (I–III).
  • Cohort I (2024): 32 participants – 12 mentors, 12 mentees, 8 peer-to-peer.
  • Cohort II (2025): 26 participants – 9 mentors, 9 mentees, 8 peer-to-peer.
  • Cohort III (2026): 42 participants – 16 mentors, 16 mentees, 8 peer-to-peer (two peer-to-peer participants dropped, final count 8).

About this dashboard

This site summarizes 2026 UX Community of Practice events and 2024–2026 mentoring cohorts. The underlying data is available in uxcop_2024_2026_events_and_mentoring.csv in this repository.
